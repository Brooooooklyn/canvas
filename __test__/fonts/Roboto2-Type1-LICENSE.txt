Roboto2-Regular.pfa and Roboto2-Regular.pfb: copied unchanged from Skia's
resources/fonts. They are Type 1 (PostScript) conversions of a subset of
Roboto (the characters of "HELLO, WORLD!"), made for Skia's tests
("Font data copyright Google 2014").
