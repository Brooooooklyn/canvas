Probe fonts of the Blink shaper fixture (blink-shaper-chrome.spec.ts). They
are made by build-probe-fonts.py in this folder with fontTools. All are
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (text below), as their sources.
A modified font must not carry a Reserved Font Name, so each modified copy
of Lato (Reserved Font Name "Lato") or Harmattan (Reserved Font Name
"Fouta", trademark "Harmattan") has its own family name. The copyright
notices of the sources are kept in each font's name table.

LoclProbe.ttf: Lato-Regular.ttf with its GSUB replaced by a locl feature
that maps "a" to another glyph per OpenType language system (ENG m, DEU w,
FRA i, JAN M, ZHS W, NLD x, TRK z, ESP k).

ScriptProbe.ttf: Lato-Regular.ttf with its GSUB replaced by a ccmp feature
that maps "1" to "m" under the DFLT script and to "w" under latn.

NominalProbe.ttf: Lato-Regular.ttf with its GSUB replaced by a ccmp
feature that maps "0" to "W" and "x" to "X" (spacing-units-chrome.spec.ts,
filter-parse-chrome.spec.ts).

LowXProbe.ttf: Lato-Regular.ttf whose cmap maps "x" to the underscore
glyph (spacing-units-chrome.spec.ts, filter-parse-chrome.spec.ts).

SymCmapProbe.ttf: Lato-Regular.ttf with only a (3,0) symbol cmap: the
glyphs of U+0020..U+00FF at U+F020..U+F0FF.

TrakProbe.ttf: Lato-Regular.ttf with an AAT trak table (track 0: +200
units at 12 pt, 0 at 24 pt, -100 at 40 pt) and a STAT table.

  Copyright (c) 2010-2011 by tyPoland Lukasz Dziedzic with Reserved Font
  Name "Lato".

ArA.ttf: Harmattan-Regular.ttf without U+0644 in its cmap.
ArB.ttf: Harmattan-Regular.ttf with unitsPerEm 1536 instead of 2048.

  Copyright (c) 2007-2008, The C&MA Guinea Fulbe Team; Copyright renewed
  2011-2012, George W. Nuss (http://www.fulbefouta.com), with the Reserved
  Font Name "Fouta". Copyright (c) 2004-2025, SIL Global.

NotoSansCJKjp-Regular-subset-halt.otf: WPT fonts/noto/cjk, as is.
ChwsProbe.otf: that file plus a chws feature that reuses its halt lookups.

  Copyright 2014-2021 Adobe (http://www.adobe.com/), with Reserved Font
  Name "Source". (Neither file carries that name.)

NotoSansDevanagari-subset.ttf: @fontsource/noto-sans-devanagari 5.3.0,
noto-sans-devanagari-devanagari-400-normal.woff2, subset to U+0020,
U+0900..U+097F, U+200C, U+200D and U+25CC (all layout features, all name
IDs) and saved as TrueType.

  Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors
  (https://github.com/notofonts/devanagari). No Reserved Font Name.

BatakBoldProbe.ttf: NotoSansDevanagari-subset.ttf (this folder) subset to
U+0020 and U+0915, renamed to the family "Noto Sans Batak" (style Bold) and
marked bold (OS/2 weight 700, fsSelection and macStyle bold): a loaded face
of a family macOS installs, without its Batak characters
(installed-fallback.spec.ts). Same copyright and license as its source.

IcWghtProbe.ttf: ScienceGothic-VariableFont.ttf (this folder) subset to
U+0020..U+007E (all layout features, all name IDs), the CTRS, slnt and
wdth axes pinned at 0, 0 and 100 with fontTools.varLib.instancer (wght
100..900 left), and U+6C34 mapped to the "M" glyph
(spacing-units-chrome.spec.ts, filter-parse-chrome.spec.ts).

  Copyright © 2025 The Science Gothic Project Authors
  (https://github.com/googlefonts/science-gothic/blob/main/AUTHORS.txt).
  No Reserved Font Name. Science Gothic is a trademark of Font Detective
  LLC; the copy is named IcWghtProbe.

IcOpszProbe.ttf: Newsreader-opsz-only.ttf (this folder) subset to
U+0020..U+007E (opsz 6..72 kept), and U+6C34 mapped to the "M" glyph
(spacing-units-chrome.spec.ts, filter-parse-chrome.spec.ts).

  Copyright 2020 The Newsreader Project Authors
  (http://github.com/productiontype/Newsreader). No Reserved Font Name.
  Newsreader is a registered trademark of Production Systems SAS; the copy
  is named IcOpszProbe.
